Limitations

  1. No counterparty identity — the binding constraint on every participant-conduct screen.
  2. C1 covers five distinct scheduled releases represented by nine settled ladders, and makes no population-level claim.
  3. C3 observes aggregate volume and open interest, not beneficial ownership.
  4. C4 scopes to strike_type = "greater" ladders only.
  5. C5 has no independent corroborating reference-data feed and performs no settlement-value divergence monitoring.
  6. Order-book reconstruction, spoofing, layering and account-level linkage are out of scope.
