identify or link accounts, establish common beneficial ownership, demonstrate
coordination, or establish intent.

16.2% and 26.0% of volume-bearing observations. Those examples show that the
signature is non-specific; they are not a corpus-wide rate or a measure of benign
activity. This market sits in the highest configured liquidity tier, but the
public data do not estimate whether position transfer is more likely in that
tier. Account and beneficial-ownership data would be needed to distinguish
potential wash activity from unrelated position transfer.
