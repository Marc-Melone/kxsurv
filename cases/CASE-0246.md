## Alternative innocent explanations

Thin or stale quoting in one of the two strikes is the ordinary cause of a ladder inversion. That is weakened here because the magnitude is several times the combined half-spread and the inversion survived a second adjacent snapshot; a stale quote typically clears within a period. A maker widening one strike while leaving its neighbour unchanged produces the same shape.

## Limitations

Kalshi's public tape carries no counterparty identity. This analysis cannot
identify or link accounts, establish common beneficial ownership, demonstrate
coordination, or establish intent.

A monotonicity violation is a statement about prices, not conduct. Public data cannot show who quoted or traded either strike, so no participant can be associated with it. The control cannot distinguish deliberate distortion of one strike from uncoordinated thin quoting.
