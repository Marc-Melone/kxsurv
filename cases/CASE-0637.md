## Limitations

Kalshi's public tape carries no counterparty identity. This analysis cannot
identify or link accounts, establish common beneficial ownership, demonstrate
coordination, or establish intent.

C1 cannot distinguish superior processing of public information from misuse of
non-public information. Surprise is weighted per trade from its own execution
price, so late converged flow is discounted rather than credited at a stale
opening quote. C1 scores 69 of 408 markets and raises 3 candidates in the saved
snapshot, but 3/69 is only a descriptive candidate rate. Small, discrete nulls
do not make the 95th-percentile rule a calibrated 5% false-positive test. The
programme covers five distinct scheduled releases represented by nine settled
ladders; ladders sharing a publication are not independent. It makes no
population-level statistical claim.
